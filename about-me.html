About Me

I’m Naiomi Summers. I’m building my skills in AI development and making software around problems I’ve dealt with in my own work.

What I’m working on now

I’m learning how to use AI in software development, from turning an idea into a working app to testing it against real workflows. Keeping my own books taught me the importance of knowing where records are stored, how to check the numbers, and how to recover the data if something goes wrong. I bring those lessons to the apps I build.

Starting a business in 2020

In 2020, I formed a C corporation for work-from-home customer service. I managed my own books while doing the customer service work. That gave me firsthand experience with the day-to-day work of running a business and keeping its records in order.

Working for myself with Amazon Flex

I also worked for myself as an Amazon Flex delivery driver and kept my own books for that work. Tracking mileage, earnings, expenses, and payouts showed me what I wanted from tools like MileQuill Lite and CurbQuill.

My technical background

I’ve worked in technical roles at school districts. That experience is part of what I bring to AI development today, alongside what I learned from running a business and working for myself.
