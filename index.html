My background

Where the apps
started.

In 2020, I started a C corporation for work-from-home customer service. After the customer calls, I still had the books to do. Keeping track of what had been billed, what had been paid, and where the records lived shaped the way I think about FirmQuill Lite and FirmQuill Full Suite.

Gig work gave me a different set of numbers to keep straight. For Uber, DoorDash, Spark, and Amazon Flex, I track miles, hours, earnings, and expenses so I have records ready at tax time. MileQuill Lite helps me look back at a shift. CurbQuill answers the next question I have when a deposit lands. Which jobs did that payment cover?

My technology experience includes technical roles in school districts. Today I’m learning to use AI in development, turning an idea into an app and testing it against the work that inspired it. That interest in records I can keep and revisit also runs through PocketQuill, my personal finance app, and DayLeaf, my private journal project.

I use AI to do the software development for my apps. I decide what I want to build, tell the AI what to change or fix, and choose what I publish.
