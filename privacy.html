Privacy

Website privacy

This static portfolio serves pages, local images, fonts, and navigation. It has no account system or form for uploading journal or business records. Each app handles its records separately.

What leaves when you choose

Opening a project, GitHub repository or source ZIP contacts that destination. Choosing an email link opens your mail program. You decide what to include and whether to send it.

Connection to the host

GitHub Pages serves the requested public files and documents that it records visitor IP addresses for security. Visiting the portfolio does not submit records from an app. External destinations have their own data practices.

GitHub Pages data collection

Managing copies and requesting help

You can clear browser history and cached site files using your browser settings. The publisher cannot clear a visitor’s profile, retrieve files already sent, or erase copies held by another recipient through this site.

For privacy questions, email NaiomiSummersDevelopment@gmail.com. Describe the question without including personal records or passwords.
