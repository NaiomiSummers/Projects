Health journal

DayLeaf

DayLeaf is a private, offline-capable web journal for recording mental health symptoms and how they affect everyday life. A check-in can be as short as two ratings. Over time, dated notes give you examples to discuss with a clinician or use in a disability review.

Data guide

Data & backups

Where records live
DayLeaf encrypts entries and drafts in this browser’s IndexedDB on this device. Your password unlocks the journal. There is no cloud account, automatic sync or password recovery.
Make a backup
Use Settings to save an encrypted .dat file with your journal, worksheets, medication history, contacts and settings. Keep the file and its password if you change devices, remove the app or clear browser data.
Restore your records
Select the .dat file and enter its original vault password. Preview the backup date and counts for each kind of record, then confirm to replace the current journal. Restored entries use the destination vault’s password.

Build a daily record

Check-ins and drafts
Choose today or a past date, rate anxiety and depression separately from 1 to 10, and fill in the details you want to keep. Drafts save as you go. The home screen tracks entry status, days logged this week and your latest weekly review.
Sleep
Enter bedtime and wake time, including overnight stretches. DayLeaf calculates time in bed. You can also record your own estimate of sleep, awakenings, naps and sleep quality.
Activities and support
Record whether you showered, changed clothes, brushed your teeth, studied or did paid work. Add minutes, breaks, help, accommodations and anything that got in the way.
Substance log and reflection
Log THC and alcohol separately with time, amount, units and notes. Describe a better, mixed or harder day in your own words and note changes in treatment or support.
Medication schedule and doses
Keep a dated history of medication starts, changes and stops with dose, time and weekdays. Mark scheduled doses taken or skipped, record effects and side effects, and separately log as-needed medication with the reason and time.

Review symptoms and support

Weekly review
Look back at seven days of ratings, sleep, activities, help, medications, substance use and treatment changes. Add what improved, what stayed difficult and what to bring up at your next appointment.
PHQ-9 and GAD-7
Complete the depression and anxiety questionnaires for the preceding two weeks. DayLeaf shows progress and completed totals and records effects on daily functioning separately.
C-SSRS self-report
Open the recent or since-last-visit suicide risk screening form, follow the applicable questions, or record completion of a paper form. Add a time, a note for your clinician or a request for support.
Personal safety plan
Save the steps you agreed on with your clinician and the names and phone numbers of people you can contact. The plan opens from the journal or a screening prompt. Tap a saved number to call. No one monitors journal entries.

Practice with the DBT workbook

Dated worksheets
Start a dialectical behavior therapy (DBT) exercise, save a draft, link it to a daily entry and return later to finish it.
Behavior Chain Analysis
Trace the events, feelings and actions surrounding a difficult moment, then identify a point where another response might help.
DEAR MAN
Prepare and rehearse a request or boundary, including how to stay focused and what you could negotiate.
DBT Diary Card
Record emotions, urges, actions and skills you tried, then describe the outcome and any support you want next time.
Check the Facts
Separate observations from your interpretation, consider other explanations and choose your next step.
Opposite Action
Explore an urge, plan a different response and record what happened when you tried it.
Pros & Cons
Compare the short- and long-term benefits and costs of acting on an urge or choosing another course.

Revisit and share entries

Entry history
Browse entries by date or filter for check-ins, weekly reviews, screens, worksheets or medication changes. Reopen a record to read it or continue a draft.
Doctor Summary
Choose a date range to summarize symptom ratings, sleep, questionnaire scores, medications, substance use and treatment changes. Select individual C-SSRS records and DBT worksheets when you want their answers included.
SSDI function summary
Prepare patient-reported examples for a Social Security Disability Insurance continuing disability review. The report shows recorded and unlogged days, changes in functioning, activities, assistance and treatment changes.
Report preview and PDF
Read the report before sharing. Download or print it, or use the device’s share sheet where supported. Exported PDFs contain readable, unencrypted health information.

Use a private journal offline

Offline readiness
Open the site online for setup and check its offline status. An installed Home Screen copy can be used without a connection after its first online launch.
Manual and automatic locking
Lock the vault from the journal. It also locks after five idle minutes and protects the journal view when you switch away.
